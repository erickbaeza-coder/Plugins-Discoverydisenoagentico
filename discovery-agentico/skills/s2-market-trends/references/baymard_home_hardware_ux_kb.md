## Home & Hardware Essentials

### Home & Hardware Essentials (16 guidelines)

- **Consider Having the First 1–2 Levels of the Site Taxonomy Implemented as Intermediary Category Pages** `#307` — Without guidance and contextual information, product finding could be troublesome for users that are not familiar with the product range. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/307)
- **Return Relevant Results for "Exact" Product Title Queries** `#333` — If a product doesn't show up when searching for its exact title or model number, users could assume the site doesn't carry the item. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/333)
- **Include Product Attributes Consistently Across List Items And Search Results of the Same Product Type** `#443` — If users can't compare similar items when in product lists and search results, they'll have to detour to product pages to find missing information, or may miss out on suitable products. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/443)
- **Support Users Searching for Compatible Products** `#341` — Users who search for accessories or parts compatible with other products, but who receive irrelevant results or results without clear compatibility guidance, may abandon or needlessly waste time attempting to verify compatibility. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/341)
- **Display "Price Per Unit" For Multiquantity Items** `#446` — Without being able to compare prices accurately, users may miss out on the best prices or may reject good-value items. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/446)
- **Always Ensure Thumbnails in Product Lists Or Search Results Make It Clear the Quantity of Products Included in Multipack Items** `#445` — Users have a very difficult time evaluating and comparing products when it isn't clear what's included in the price. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/445)
- **Include an "Accessories" Subcategory within Relevant Categories** `#268` — For some users, it's very difficult to find accessories for products, while other users who are uninterested in accessories are often faced with "accessory pollution" in product lists. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/268)
- **Consider Including the Main Navigation Categories Directly on the Homepage** `#236` — In some industries, category browsing generally performs better than other product-finding strategies, yet users often aren't sufficiently encouraged to adopt the strategy on the homepage. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/236)
- **Provide Category-Specific Filters for All Key Product Attributes** `#409` — Users may be unable to find suitable products if they can't filter by attributes that differentiate them from others. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/409)
- **Always Explain Industry-Specific Filters** `#433` — Filter types and options that contain industry jargon can be difficult to understand and can lead to users missing out on suitable items. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/433)
- **Dynamically Adapt Product List and Search Results Layouts to a "List View" or "Grid View" for Mixed Product Catalogs** `#533` — Using a single list or search results layout (a "List View" or a "Grid View") for both spec-driven and visually driven products creates difficulties for users in evaluating and comparing options. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/533)
- **Provide Supplementary Product Information Like Manuals and Production Ethics** `#807` — Users with special purchase preferences or requirements will often need extremely detailed product information to verify the suitability of a product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/807)
- **Ensure a Consistently High Level of Detail in the Product Descriptions** `#841` — In the absence of expected or desired product information, users will often assume a product does not have the specific attribute if the information is not present. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/841)
- **Use Both "Bullet Lists" and "Blocks of Text" for Product Descriptions** `#843` — Users have different approaches to searching for product information based on their information-gathering style, where they are in their purchasing decision, and the extent of their product familiarity, and need to be able to both scan and read product descriptions. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/843)
- **Highlight Key Product Features in the Product Headline** `#860` — Product headlines that only include the product title don't give users an overview of the product during the critical first few seconds after they arrive on the product page. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/860)
- **Display Warranty Upgrades Directly on the Product Details Page** `#819` — Basic product warranty information may sometimes fall short for users who incorporate extended warranties and service support into their decision to buy a product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/819)

## Product Page

### Specifications Sheet (5 guidelines)

- **Provide Core Product Info Both in the Product Description and in the Spec Sheet** `#858` — Users look for key product information in both the product descriptions and the product specification sheet, and users looking in one location may miss out on vital information that is only located in the other. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/858)
- **Explain or Define Most Specification Labels in the Spec Sheet** `#840` — Unfamiliar terminology and industry-specific jargon can prevent users from clearly understanding product specifications. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/840)
- **How to Improve the Scannability of the Spec Sheet** `#835` — Pinpointing specific information is difficult on disorganized and text-heavy spec sheets. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/835)
- **Harmonize Vendor-Supplied Specs to Ensure Consistency and Uniformity** `#839` — It's impossible for users to directly compare products in spec sheets when they're faced with inconsistent product information, such as specifications described with different units of measurement, labels, or level of detail. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/839)
- **Always Append the "Unit of Measure" to All Numerical Spec Values** `#857` — Values for numerical specs are indistinguishable by themselves, and are prone to misinterpretation. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/857)

### Product Images (11 guidelines)

- **Always Provide at Least 3 Product Images for All Products** `#740` — Users heavily rely on a variety of product images to inspect and evaluate products and decide if the product matches what they are seeking, but are sometimes not provided with sufficient visual information. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/740)
- **Always Provide 'In Scale' Product Images** `#741` — Users are unable to get a sense of or evaluate a product's size when only provided with "Cut Out" product images. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/741)
- **Always Provide a "Dimensions" Image for Relevant Products** `#2510` — Product dimensions communicated through text can be difficult to interpret, increasing the likelihood of abandonment when a product's exact size is critical to determining its suitability. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2510)
- **Ensure All Main Gallery Product Images Are of Sufficient Quality** `#779` — Users need to see crucial product details to fully visually inspect products, and if they're unable to, they may abandon an otherwise suitable product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/779)
- **Include All Key Site-Provided Product Images in the Main Image Gallery** `#775` — Users can overlook images that are displayed in unexpected locations on the product page, which can limit their visual exploration of the product and impair their judgment of whether the product suits their needs. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/775)
- **Provide Unique Versions of All Product Images for Each Visual Variation** `#853` — Users aren't provided with all the visual information they need if they can't see enough images of the product in their preferred visual variation. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/853)
- **Consider Providing "Feature Callout" Images for Key Product Features** `#747` — Users focus their initial product evaluation on product images, which may lead them to overlook key product features that are only described in the product description and specifications. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/747)
- **Consider Providing 3 or More Images of Products from Multiple Angles** `#755` — Users need to gain a comprehensive visual understanding of a product in order to make a purchase decision, and straight-on shots of the product rarely fulfill this requirement. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/755)
- **Always Provide an "Included Accessories" Image for Relevant Products** `#745` — Users can be unsure what accessories are available for products and whether they're included in the purchase price. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/745)
- **Consider Including Additional Descriptive Text for Some Product Images** `#752` — Product images can be difficult for users to decode, in particular when trying to understand features or specifications. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/752)
- **Consider Integrating Social Media Visuals on the Product Page** `#2536` — For some products, users expect images from a variety of sources to help them assess the product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2536)

### Product Videos & 360-Views (4 guidelines)

- **Use Video to Showcase Products** `#759` — Important product features can be difficult to convey using static images alone. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/759)
- **Always Place Site-Provided Product Videos in the Main Image Gallery** `#770` — Users who overlook product videos can miss out on critical product information. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/770)
- **Ensure Product Videos are Fast Loading and Have Scrubbing Previews and Descriptive Titles** `#760` — Users have high expectations for site-provided video controls and features and may miss out on critical product information contained in videos when they are not met. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/760)
- **Consider Providing a 360-View Feature** `#761` — 360-View features can provide an unlimited number of angle views, and are greatly appreciated by a minority of users, but many users either won't use them or won't understand them. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/761)

### Product Descriptions (12 guidelines)

- **Ensure a Consistently High Level of Detail in the Product Descriptions** `#841` — In the absence of expected or desired product information, users will often assume a product does not have the specific attribute if the information is not present. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/841)
- **Use Both "Bullet Lists" and "Blocks of Text" for Product Descriptions** `#843` — Users have different approaches to searching for product information based on their information-gathering style, where they are in their purchasing decision, and the extent of their product familiarity, and need to be able to both scan and read product descriptions. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/843)
- **Place the Product Description within 1–2 Viewports of the End of the "Buy" Section on Mobile** `#1226` — Mobile page designs can make product descriptions — crucial to users' ability to make a purchase decision — hard to find, especially if "One Long Page" is used. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/1226)
- **Provide an AI-Generated Product Description Overview** `#3333` — Comprehensive product descriptions can often consist of an overwhelming amount of information for some users, increasing the time and effort to consume and interpret it. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3333)
- **Consider Structuring Product Descriptions by 'Product Highlights'** `#861` — It's difficult for some users to find, understand and be inspired by products described only in text blocks or bullet lists. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/861)
- **Highlight Key Product Features in the Product Headline** `#860` — Product headlines that only include the product title don't give users an overview of the product during the critical first few seconds after they arrive on the product page. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/860)
- **Subdivide Long Product Descriptions with Sections and Headings** `#845` — Long, unbroken, and unstructured product descriptions can be intimidating and make it difficult to locate specific types of information. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/845)
- **Always Provide Specific and Detailed Material and Care Information for Relevant Products** `#2862` — Users often encounter missing, insufficient, or generic information about material and care for products when these details are crucial to the purchase decision. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2862)
- **Use Straightforward Language in Product Descriptions** `#844` — Users without technical or industry knowledge are unable to immediately grasp technical information and industry-specific jargon in the product description and thus will not understand the basic features of a product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/844)
- **Always Provide Explanations for All Complex Product Configuration Options** `#3260` — Without full knowledge of every feature of a product with complex configurations, users may hesitate to buy. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3260)
- **Avoid Excessive Use of "Marketing Copy" in the Product Description** `#1238` — Users feel "oversold" by excessive marketing copy, which can in turn damage the credibility of claims made about products, and distract users from learning about a product's key features. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/1238)
- **Consider Including Some of the Product Description 'Above the Fold'** `#847` — Some users will make partially informed judgements about product suitability based on the information above the fold of the product details page. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/847)

### Shipping, Returns, and Gifting (6 guidelines)

- **Provide a "Total Order Cost" Estimate Near the "Buy" Section** `#825` — Knowing the total order cost, or at least having an idea about it, will for most users be a critical part of the product-purchasing decision. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/825)
- **Make the Essential Conditions for "Free Shipping" Offers Visible by Default** `#826` — Users who see conditional offers for "free shipping" on the product page — but don't see or understand the offer's conditions — may feel "tricked" at checkout if shipping isn't free. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/826)
- **Display or Link to the Return Policy from the Main Product Page Content** `#803` — Users unsure about products they are considering must spend extra time locating the return policy when it isn't easily available. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/803)
- **Place "Free Shipping" Information In or Near the "Buy" Section** `#836` — "Free Shipping" information is very valuable to users, but they must be able to find it on the product page to benefit from it. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/836)
- **Include "Find in Store" Information Near the "Buy" Section** `#804` — Missing or inadequate "Find in Store" information makes it more difficult for users to locate a nearby retail store where they can evaluate potential purchases in person. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/804)
- **Provide an Estimated Delivery Date or Range Near the "Buy" Section** `#802` — Many users don't want to wait until checkout to know when they'll receive their order, and "Delivery Speed" requires users estimate themselves when they can expect to receive their order. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/802)

### In-Page Navigation (9 guidelines)

- **Avoid Using "Horizontal Tabs" for the Main Product Page Sections** `#753` — "Horizontal Tabs" make core product page content harder to find on both desktop and mobile devices. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/753)
- **On Shorter Product Pages, Consider Using an "Expanded Sections" Layout** `#757` — Collapsed and tabbed product page sections add unnecessary cognitive and physical effort for users navigating within shorter product pages. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/757)
- **Consider Including a "Sticky ToC" on Longer Desktop Product Pages Using the "Expanded Sections" Layout** `#784` — Scrolling can add unnecessary friction when users want to navigate directly to a specific section within longer product pages using the "Expanded Sections" layout. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/784)
- **On Longer Mobile Product Pages, Consider Using a "Collapsed Sections" Layout** `#1129` — The limited viewport on mobile can make it harder for users to find information within longer product pages. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/1129)
- **If Using a "Collapsed Sections" Layout, Expand the "Product Description" and "User Reviews" Sections** `#3006` — The "Collapsed Sections" layout conceals product information by default, which can make it more difficult for some users to find key content sections on product pages. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3006)
- **Always Provide an Item Count for Hidden Product Page Sections Containing Dynamically Generated Content** `#3003` — Users cannot predict whether dynamically populated sections hidden by default will contain any information, or how much information they contain, before accessing them. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3003)
- **Always Fade the Content Immediately Preceding Truncated Content** `#846` — Overlooking truncated content will leave some users without the information they need to make a purchase decision. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/846)
- **Use Accurate and Specific Section Titles for Product Page Content** `#766` — Inaccurate or overly generic content section titles can make it difficult for users to locate and navigate to relevant information on the product page. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/766)
- **Avoid Using "Split-Scrolling" Product Page Layouts** `#2868` — "Split-scrolling", two-column layout pattern variations increase the effort required to scroll vertically through a PDP, generating user confusion and frustration and heightening the risk of site abandonment. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2868)

### Image Gallery UI (11 guidelines)

- **Always Use Thumbnails in Image Galleries on Desktop** `#774` — Desktop users overlook additional images and have no information scent when images are represented by indicator dots or text, or only navigation arrows are provided. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/774)
- **Always Provide Sufficient Zoom Magnification in the Main Image Gallery on Desktop** `#2997` — Insufficient magnification in the desktop image gallery zoom prevents users from accessing the required level of visual detail. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2997)
- **Always Provide Single-Click-Activated Zoom in Desktop "Image Gallery" Overlays** `#2994` — The default main image view does not give users access to the expected additional visual detail in the "Image Gallery" overlay. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2994)
- **Ensure the Main Product Image and Swatches Are Visible in the Same Mobile Viewport** `#1217` — Users on mobile who have to repeatedly scroll up and down to view "Preview Images" after selecting different color swatches find the process troublesome and frustrating. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/1217)
- **Always Signpost Hidden Thumbnails in Image Galleries** `#2875` — Providing no indication of hidden thumbnails makes it much more likely that users will overlook additional gallery images. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2875)
- **Always Provide a Sufficiently Large Main Image in the "Image Gallery" Overlay** `#2952` — Small images in desktop "Image Gallery" overlays increase the effort required by users to extract the additional visual detail they expect to find. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2952)
- **Always Provide Clear Exits in "Image Gallery" Overlays** `#2940` — Doubt about how to return to the product page from desktop "Image Gallery" overlays adds time and effort for users. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2940)
- **Always Provide Hover-Activated Zoom in the Main Image Gallery** `#781` — The default level of detail in main gallery images is sometimes insufficient to allow users to evaluate a product. And click-activated zoom is more likely to be overlooked by desktop users than hover-activated. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/781)
- **Use an External Zoom Window in the Main Image Gallery** `#2991` — Users can struggle to navigate to the detail they need within zoomed images. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2991)
- **Consider Using Thumbnails in Image Galleries on Mobile** `#2884` — Mobile users will swipe to access gallery images regardless of whether thumbnails or other indicator types are used, but the meaning of indicator dots can be ambiguous leading to some minor confusion. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2884)
- **Consider Scaling Gallery Images Proportionally When Switching to Landscape Mode on Mobile** `#1488` — Users may turn their mobile device to landscape mode in order to see larger, more detailed images and will be disappointed if the image isn't as large as they expected. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/1488)

### The "Buy" Section (10 guidelines)

- **Style the Primary "Add to Cart" Button to Be Unique and Prominent** `#791` — Competing call to action buttons are distracting and make it needlessly difficult for users to add the product to their cart. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/791)
- **Allow Users to Purchase "Out of Stock" Products by Increasing the Delivery Time** `#795` — Temporarily unavailable or out of stock products leave users at a dead end, and users who are decided on a particular product are then very likely to go to a competing site. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/795)
- **Promote Alternatives for Discontinued Products** `#820` — If a user's desired product is unavailable for purchase, they may abandon a site if they can't easily find a suitable alternative product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/820)
- **How to Implement 'Save' Features** `#798` — The significant subgroup of users who rely on 'Save' and 'Wishlist' features will struggle to save products when the feature requires an account to use or is difficult to access or understand. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/798)
- **Display "Price Per Unit" for Products Sold in Varying Quantities or Amounts** `#817` — Products sold in multiquantity packaging or varying sizes make direct comparison more difficult, leading to misinterpretation of products' relative value. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/817)
- **Style Product Price to Be Highly Visible** `#792` — Without a clear price, users cannot evaluate a critical aspect of the product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/792)
- **How to Display Price-Related Special Offers and Discounts** `#816` — Sales pricing and discounts can entice a user to buy, but unclear or inconsistent pricing information can be a deterrent. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/816)
- **Consider Hiding the Quantity Field for Some Product Types** `#793` — Users can be distracted by quantity fields, which can also cause interaction issues. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/793)
- **Consider Focusing the "Buy" Section on Only Essential Information and Options** `#789` — Including too much information or many options in the "Buy" section can overwhelm users and distract them from adding products to their cart. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/789)
- **Display Relevant Order Details on Product Pages for Signed-In Users** `#884` — Failing to personalize the product details page for signed-in users makes it harder for users to repurchase products and manage other order-related information. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/884)

### Product Variations (12 guidelines)

- **Reproduce Core Content Across All Product Variations** `#848` — When core product content is stretched thinly across multiple product variations, it's harder for users to gather all the information they need to make an informed purchase decision. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/848)
- **Always Use Swatches or Thumbnails as Selectors for Color Variations** `#849` — When users cannot easily determine all the color variations a product comes in they may overlook their preferred options, leading them to prematurely abandon suitable products. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/849)
- **Always Include All Variations of a Product on the Same Product Page** `#3009` — Users may not discover product variants if they are contained on separate product pages. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3009)
- **Always Provide a Preview of Visually Customized Products** `#800` — Users may hesitate to purchase a visually customized — and likely nonreturnable — product if they're not confident about how their desired customization will look. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/800)
- **Always Use Individual "Buttons" for Size and Other Non-Color Variations** `#850` — Some users will either miss size (or other non-color) variations which are not immediately and directly visible, or will need to spend additional time and effort locating and evaluating this critical information about product options. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/850)
- **Preselect the Product Variation Users Have Filtered For** `#852` — Users can become disoriented when product list information, such as the product thumbnail and applied filters like size and color, is not maintained once they navigate to the product page. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/852)
- **Once a Size or Color Has Been Selected, Always Indicate Unavailable Variations** `#2709` — Some users discover that colors that appear available for their selected size are in fact not available. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2709)
- **Provide Product Page Color Filters for Products with Many Color Variations** `#3254` — It's very difficult for users to compare lots of similar colors when they are spread throughout a long list containing many unsuitable shades. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3254)
- **Always Provide Permanently Visible Text Labels Near Thumbnail and Swatch Color Selectors** `#3015` — When text descriptions are overlooked, color variation selectors alone may not provide users with sufficient information to quickly understand differences between subtle color variations. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3015)
- **Use Prominent Diagonal Slashes to Clearly Indicate Unavailable Product Variants** `#3012` — Without clearly presented stock status information, users spend additional time establishing whether a product is available in a suitable variant. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3012)
- **If Offering Extensive Product-Configuration Options, Consider Providing a "Configuration Summary" at the End of the Configuration Process** `#3257` — Users can lose track of the options they have chosen in a complex configuration process. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3257)
- **Always Persist a Selected, Available Size across Color Variations** `#2712` — When users select an available size for an item, the size deselects when they click or tap to view different available colors. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2712)

### User Reviews (20 guidelines)

- **Always Allow Users to Submit Product Reviews** `#739` — Users heavily depend on user-generated reviews. Without them, many users will not be confident enough to make a decision about a product and will go off-site to look for more information. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/739)
- **Never Have an Account Wall for Submitting Product Reviews** `#743` — Too few reviews will hinder users' ability to seek patterns, find relevant information, and may lead users to doubt the reviews' authenticity. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/743)
- **Provide an AI-Generated Reviews Summary** `#3336` — Traditional review tools (e.g., filter, sort, and search) can be cumbersome, especially for users seeking a quick, high-level understanding of user feedback before they invest more time and effort to evaluate a product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3336)
- **Avoid Truncating the Full AI-Generated Reviews Summary** `#3339` — Truncating the full AI-generated reviews summary risks a subgroup of users falsely concluding that the visible summarized feedback represents all the reviews, rather than just a portion of them. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3339)
- **Provide Search for User Reviews** `#3345` — Users may have very particular concerns about a product that are not clearly addressed by an AI-generated reviews summary or filtering and sorting tools. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3345)
- **Always Highlight Matched Search Keywords or Filtered Terms in User Reviews** `#3429` — Scanning reviews to find text that mentions the keywords a user has searched or filtered for can be tedious and mentally cumbersome, particularly in lengthy reviews. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3429)
- **Provide AI- or Machine-Generated "Keyword" Filters with Reviews** `#3348` — It can be difficult for users to find reviews that address their specific concerns when there are hundreds or thousands of reviews. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3348)
- **Always Categorize "Keyword" Review Filters as Positive or Negative** `#3438` — Lack of adequate up-front information about the sentiment and scope of associated reviews undermines the value of AI- and machine-generated "Keyword" filters. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/3438)
- **Allow and Encourage Users to Upload Images with Their Review** `#756` — Text-only reviews don't include key information that past buyers have about the product. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/756)
- **Always Provide a Gallery of Reviewer-Submitted Images at the Top of the Reviews Section** `#2557` — Users aren't always aware when reviewer-submitted images are available in the reviews section. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2557)
- **Always Allow Users to Navigate across User Reviews Using Reviewer-Submitted Images** `#2554` — Users have difficulty viewing all reviewer-submitted images in the reviews section. · [↗](https://baymard.com/premium/guidelines/2554)
